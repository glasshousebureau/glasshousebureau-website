About the Studio

Why We Exist

Designed environments
for genuine growth.

A glasshouse doesn't fight the weather. It creates the right conditions inside so that what you are growing has a real chance. That is the idea behind everything we do.

Glasshouse Studio is an inclusive design and business strategy studio founded by Jacob Cogan and Daniel Goerz. We are designers, builders, and strategists who believe the world needs more businesses that exist for a reason — not just a market gap, but a genuine reason to be.

We kept seeing the same problem: organizations with good intentions building systems that quietly left people out. Not out of malice — out of habit, speed, and designing for an imaginary average person who does not actually exist.

Designing for the edges makes everything stronger. That is not an idealistic position — it is a practical one.

When you start with the people a system has failed, you end up building something better for everyone. Our work combines strategy, design, technology, and operational thinking — brought together in a way that feels collaborative rather than transactional, because the best outcomes we have been part of happened when we were genuinely inside the problem.

The Founders

01

Jacob Cogan

Co-Founder & Design Lead

Jacob is an industrial designer, product leader, projection mapper, artist, and community builder. His entry point into design was personal — growing up with learning disabilities gave him an early, lived understanding of what it feels like when systems are not built for you. That experience became the foundation of everything he makes.

His career spans product design, accessibility infrastructure, community-centred experiences, and interdisciplinary making. He thinks of accessibility not as a compliance checkbox but as the starting point for design that is genuinely useful — the kind that holds up for the full range of people who encounter it.

Jacob brings a maker's instinct to every problem: the belief that the right thing to do is usually to build something and see what it teaches you.

Daniel Goerz

Co-Founder & Strategy Lead

Daniel built his first company from the ground up — with a clear vision, limited resources, and an idea ambitious enough that nothing about its success was guaranteed. That company is now an international business generating multi-million dollars in annual revenue, having fundamentally changed how students with disabilities learn, attracting technology partners and global resellers that brought it to markets across multiple continents.

Along the way, that business acquired and turned around smaller businesses — improving operations, tightening margins, and rebuilding cultures that people actually wanted to work inside of. Whether the organization is large or small, his instinct is the same: find where the system is working against itself, fix the underlying structure, and make sure the people inside it have room to do their best work. He believes strong companies are built by treating people with respect, recognizing substance over volume, and not overlooking the quieter employees who are often delivering the most value while others dominate the room.

His academic background spans behavioural support, human development, educational psychology, and applied behaviour analysis — which in practice means he thinks carefully about how people actually move through systems, and where the friction is costing more than anyone realizes. Glasshouse Studio is where all of that experience comes together.

Values & Formed Perspective

The work we do is shaped by
how we have chosen to live.

Jacob Cogan

Contemplative Practice

Jacob has practiced within the Theravāda Buddhist tradition for years, including extended time at monasteries in Ontario and British Columbia. It has shaped a simple working principle: understand what is actually there before you reach for a solution.

Daniel Goerz

Faith Formation & Service

Daniel spent over a decade in structured faith formation, volunteering thousands of hours in direct community service and delivering public discourses to multilingual congregations across seven languages. That background is foundational to how he thinks about service, communication, and who any given system is actually built for.

Six years.
Different strengths.

Jacob and Daniel met in Toronto and have been designing together for over six years. What started as a shared passion for empathetic, people-centred design became a genuine creative partnership — built on trust, complementary skills, and a working chemistry that consistently produces better output than either would arrive at alone.

They share a love of art, a deep connection to the people they design for, and a commitment to showing up as the best version of themselves in the work. On every engagement, you get both of them — not a pitch from the founders followed by a handoff to junior staff.
