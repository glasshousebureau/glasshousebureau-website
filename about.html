About the Studio

Built from
lived experience.

Glasshouse Studio is an inclusive design and business strategy studio founded by Jacob Cogan and Irvin Hale. We are designers, builders, and strategists who believe the world needs more businesses that exist for a reason beyond selling things people can be convinced they need.

We work with people who want to build something better — and with organizations that are ready to stop designing for the average person and start designing for everyone.

What We Believe

Designing for the edges makes
everything stronger.

Most businesses are designed for the average customer. The problem is the average customer does not really exist.

When you start with the people a system has failed — the ones who are overwhelmed, excluded, working around something that was never built for them — you end up building something stronger for everyone. Not as a gesture toward inclusion, but because designing for real human variation produces better outcomes than designing for a fictional average.

Glasshouse Studio exists for purpose-driven founders and organizations who want to build businesses and systems that are commercially real and genuinely useful to the world — without having to learn every hard lesson from scratch.

The Founders

Two founders.
One shared conviction.

01

Jacob
Cogan

Co-Founder

Jacob is an industrial designer, product leader, projection mapper, artist, and community builder. As VP of Product at Habitat Learn, he helped design Messenger Pigeon from its foundations — drawing directly from his lived experience with learning disabilities to build a tool around how attention, memory, and learning actually work, not how institutions assume they work.

His background in industrial design informs how he approaches every project: as systems that exist in bodies, spaces, materials, and communities — not just on screens. His creative practice spans painting, software tools, projection mapping, TouchDesigner experiences, and interdisciplinary workshops in Ottawa.

02

Irvin
Hale

Co-Founder

Irvin Hale is an accessibility entrepreneur, strategist, and founder. As CEO and co-founder of a multi-million dollar EdTech company and Apple ecosystem partner — he has worked closely with colleges, universities, disability service offices, students, faculty, and institutional executives to redesign how post-secondary institutions support students.

His academic background spans behavioral support, human development, educational psychology, and applied behaviour analysis. Before building companies, he spent thousands of hours doing volunteer work and public speaking to multilingual audiences — work that shaped how he thinks about trust, communication, and what it means to design for someone who has never seen themselves reflected in the systems they are asked to use.

Why Glasshouse

A designed environment
for growth.

A glasshouse protects what is emerging while giving it the conditions it needs to become stronger. It is structured, but alive. Intentional, but adaptive. It blurs the line between inside and outside — creating something that is neither purely shelter nor purely nature, but something more honest than either.

Good design does not force one way of being. It makes room for difference. It creates the conditions for people, ideas, products, and organizations to grow — regardless of what is happening outside. Glasshouse Studio exists to help build those conditions.

For the businesses that should exist but do not yet. For the institutions that want to do better and need help getting there. For the people who have been working around broken systems long enough.

"When you design for the edges of human experience, you almost always make something better for everyone. That is not a trade-off. That is the work."
