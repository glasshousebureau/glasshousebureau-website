Our Thinking

A glasshouse is a designed environment for growth. The weather outside is unpredictable. What matters is whether what you are building has the right conditions inside to thrive.

We work with founders, public servants, business owners, faith leaders, and institutions designing products, services, and ways of operating that feel grounded in where things are actually heading — not where they used to be.

Our work combines strategy, design, technology, and operational thinking, brought together in a way that feels collaborative rather than transactional. Jacob and Daniel are genuine creative partners — different strengths, shared instincts, and a working chemistry that makes the output noticeably better. You get both of us on every engagement.

Services

Six disciplines
01

Designing for the people who will actually use it.

Most products and systems are designed for an imaginary average customer who does not really exist. We design for the real range of people who will live with what you are making — which produces better outcomes, more loyalty, and far fewer expensive misses. We are particularly drawn to founders building outside the lines and public sector teams who genuinely care about the citizens they serve.

02

Building products and platforms that work in the real world.

Websites, apps, internal tools, customer experiences. We work across the full lifecycle — figuring out what should exist, designing it, building it, and making sure it keeps working after launch.

03

Helping teams operate smarter — without losing what makes them good.

Efficiency has been getting a bad name lately, and for good reason. We are not here to help anyone downsize for the sake of it. We help organizations use technology, design, and clear thinking to free their teams up — so people get to do their best work, with more room left over for the lives they actually want.

04

Bringing AI into your work in a way that ages well.

We favour smaller, offline, on-device models built for specific use cases — less energy, better privacy, no data leaving the room. More importantly, we help organizations use AI to amplify what their people are good at: the creativity, the judgment, the human parts of the work that no model is going to replace.

05

Helping founders build businesses that hold up.

Strategy, positioning, business model design, go-to-market thinking, grant identification, and the kind of honest feedback that is hard to get when everyone around you is being polite. We have built companies. We know what tends to work and what tends to fall apart.

06

Redesigning institutional systems so they actually serve people.

Public sector institutions, universities, and large organizations often have the intention to serve everyone — but the systems they have built tell a different story. We help teams move from good intentions to genuine, working implementation.
